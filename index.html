CHW voice pipeline

Community health workers in Kenya record referrals on the MOH-100 form. In practice the form is paper, the data arrives late, and the smartphone app that was supposed to fix it assumes literacy, battery and bundles that the last mile does not have.

This pipeline takes a voice note in the worker's own language, transcribes it, extracts the 22 MOH-100 fields, and writes a structured row a supervisor can act on the same day. Cost per referral is under one Kenyan shilling, computed from metered usage rather than asserted. Records the model was unsure about are routed to a human instead of guessed.

Epidemic intelligence

Outbreak analytics built around the four ways outbreak dashboards mislead people: the epidemic curve that always falls at the right-hand edge because recent cases have not been reported yet, the naive case fatality ratio briefed mid-response, the reproduction number quoted as a point estimate at low counts, and no way to reconstruct what was known on a past date.

Every estimator implements a published method and cites it. The repository includes the product requirements document, because the design decisions are as much the point as the implementation.
